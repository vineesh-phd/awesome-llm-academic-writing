# Datasets

- **arXiv Dataset (Kaggle bulk metadata)**
  Metadata and abstracts for 2M+ arXiv papers, updated regularly.
  Source: Cornell University / Kaggle — [Link](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/Cornell-University/arxiv)
  Used by several of the stylistic/lexical-shift papers in this repository (e.g., Geng & Trotta 2024, Bao et al. 2025) to detect ChatGPT-era writing-style changes across arXiv abstracts.

- **OpenAlex**
  Open, comprehensive scholarly metadata graph covering hundreds of millions of works.
  Source: OpenAlex — [Link](https://openalex.org/)
  The 2.8-million-article corpus behind Lin et al.'s (2025) causal lexical-shift study — usable for replicating or extending that analysis to other disciplines or time windows.

- **CHEAT: A Large-scale Dataset for Detecting ChatGPT-writtEn AbsTracts**
  35,304 synthetic abstracts (Generation, Polish, and Mix variants) paired with originals, purpose-built for training/evaluating AI-text detectors on academic content.
  Source: Yu, Chen, Feng, & Xia (2023), arXiv:2304.12008 — [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12008)
  Directly supports the Ethics/Authorship & Integrity category — a ready-made benchmark for testing detection tools against academic-style AI writing.
